Future-State Journey: One Balance, One Due Date

Riverty is extending from pay-later checkout into a consumer card for its heaviest BNPL users, by replacing the habit of checking several apps with one balance.

PersonaHeavy BNPL user, 18-34, runs two pay-later apps (N-01, N-02, N-04)
GoalEveryday spend, including splitting the big weekly shop, without another app to monitor
Friction replacedTwo screens, two reminders, no single total (N-07, T-103, T-104)
  1. 1

    Activate & Add to Wallet

    User Action
    Add card to wallet at approval -> tap-to-pay ready immediately
    Internal State (inferred)
    Eager to try it; low patience for setup
    Pain Point Addressed
    Wallet add inside approval (B2) -> fixes 143 of 431 cards never added (T-102)
  2. 2

    Pay in Shop

    User Action
    Tap card at supermarket -> split the weekly shop, no extra app
    Internal State (inferred)
    Expects debit-card reliability (N-03)
    Pain Point Addressed
    Decline reason and retry hint (B1) -> no silent switch to debit (T-101, N-06)
  3. 3

    Check One Balance

    User Action
    Open app before payday -> see card and invoices as one total
    Internal State (inferred)
    In control; one look settles the question (N-02)
    Pain Point Addressed
    One balance view (B3) -> ends "which total is real" (T-103, N-07)
  4. 4

    Settle on One Due Date

    User Action
    Receive one reminder -> pay one amount on one date
    Internal State (inferred)
    Confident the amount is right (T-104)
    Pain Point Addressed
    One due date (B8) -> replaces two conflicting reminders (T-104)

Competitive Advantages over the Manual Workaround

  1. 1One balance -> no summing across apps before payday
  2. 2One due date -> no reconciling mismatched reminders
  3. 3Clear decline reasons -> no silent fallback to the debit card

Internal States are inferred from cited notes and unvalidated. Workaround steps are hypotheses; the pack records no external tools.

B8 may alter terms and trigger months of re-approval (N-11). Merchant offers (B5) are outside this persona's journey. Source: fictional practice data pack, not Riverty facts.
