# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** Business Unit - Pay & Credit
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Inaction over 12 months leads to slow erosion, not a sudden collapse. The case rests on the direct relationship, retention versus rivals, merchant leverage, and credit or installment income.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Uses an existing card, or a competitor card (Klarna Card, PayPal).
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Riverty’s heaviest BNPL users (about €321 a month in the pilot) return to their debit card after a single unexplained decline or a split balance, so every month without a reliable, unified card leaves the everyday consumer relationship to Klarna and PayPal while merchants remain Riverty’s only growth lever.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For heavy online BNPL users like Lena (about €321 a month in the pilot)
who want money to leave their account only after they decide to keep an item,
Riverty Card is a pay-later card that works beyond checkout, with one balance and one due date for card and pay later orders and a clear reason whenever a payment fails.
Unlike Klarna and PayPal, which sit alongside Riverty as separate apps, it gives one place to see what you owe and keeps Riverty’s partner merchants at the centre of spend
