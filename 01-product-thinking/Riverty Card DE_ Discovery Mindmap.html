Riverty Card, Germany

Discovery sprint mindmap. As of 9 Nov 2026. Card spec not public: all product assumptions are labeled.

Primary user (hypothesis)

Digital-native German adult, debit/girocard and PayPal-first, already uses pay-later online. Wants to spread larger purchases and see what they owe, without a credit-card approval wall or Schufa penalty.

Grounding and gaps
  • FRiverty received an EU banking licence (Luxembourg) in May 2026. German pay-later relationships moved to Riverty Bank S.A. on 1 Aug 2026 (secondary source, verify internally).
  • FCCD II applies from Nov 2026 and extends credit rules to short-term and interest-free instalments.
  • FGermany remains a debit-card market. Credit cards are often a secondary card for travel, hotels, online.
  • FCompetitors move: BBVA launched a German credit card in Jun 2026 (5% cashback for 6 months, capped €150). Girocard gets in-app merchant wallet features from spring 2027. Schufa counts account and card inquiries in its score since Mar 2026.
  • FStatista summary describes Riverty consumer adoption in Germany as not significant (figure paywalled).
Gaps before building. (1) Card type undefined: debit, charge, revolving or pay-later-linked. Branches 3 to 5 change materially by type. (2) No primary research in hand: every job below is a hypothesis. (3) Unit economics need internal data (loss rates, CAC). Sprint output should be falsifiable tests, not a feature list.
1

Core jobs-to-be-done
  • J1 Split a big purchase, anywhere HCore When a purchase exceeds this month’s buffer, let me spread it at any merchant, so I stay in control.
  • J2 One place for what I owe HCore When I shop across merchants, show all obligations and due dates in one view.
  • J3 Meet the “credit card required” moment HWedge Hotels recommend a card on file at booking or check-in (FIHA). Give me one without a credit-wall.
  • J4 Apply without hurting my score HEnabler Card inquiries count in Schufa since Mar 2026 (F). Show limit before a hard inquiry.
  • J5 Pay safely online and in store Hygiene Wallet, 3-DS, instant freeze. Required, not differentiating.
⚑ T1: Everyday card or situational card? J1 to J4 describe a situational card. A top-of-wallet card must beat girocard and debit habits and needs rewards. Choose one positioning; it drives every branch below.
2

Friction in existing behavior
  • Debit-first habit FCredit card is rarely the default; activation is the hard problem, not issuance.
  • Fragmented pay-later HOne invoice or app per merchant. Validate frequency in interviews.
  • Approval anxiety FInquiry count now feeds the Schufa score.
  • Cost opacity FPress regularly warns about “cost traps” of free cards. Transparency is a trust lever.
  • Low consumer brand pull FHStatista signals low adoption; Riverty is likely known through merchant checkout, not as a consumer brand. Test awareness.
  • Convenience gap is closing FGirocard in-app wallet features (spring 2027) erode “easy online payment” as a wedge.

Sprint plan: test the riskiest assumptions first

1. Decide card type and positioning (T1)

Decision workshop, day 1 to 2. Blocks everything else.

2. Validate primary user and J1 to J4

Interviews with existing Riverty pay-later users in Germany. Rank jobs by importance and satisfaction.

3. Quantify cannibalization (T3)

Internal data: overlap between card-eligible users and merchant checkout volume.

4. Unit economics model (T4)

Interchange cap vs expected loss vs acquisition cost. Needs internal inputs.

5. Legal and compliance scan

CCD II, limit preview without hard inquiry, scoring data use, passporting.

6. Concept test positioning

Situational vs everyday card message, fake-door or prototype.
